Vendor cloud accounts

Cloud credentials for third-party smart-home vendors (Tapo, Hue, Nest, Ring, …). One row per cloud account drives many devices on that account. Secrets are Fernet-encrypted at rest; plaintext only appears in memory at firmware-bundle build time. New plaintext is shown once on entry — copy + paste from your vendor's console rather than re-typing.
